LEGAL POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026. Learn about how we handle, process, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect credentials you register with (e.g. email address, first name, last name). For polls, we store choices selected and recorded timestamps. Individual voter identity is protected through rigorous authorization boundaries.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to power core VoteHub capabilities, including:

  • Authenticating sessions and securing accounts.
  • Tracking whether a voter has already participated in a specific program.
  • Compiling aggregate, anonymous survey and stats data for dashboard display.

3. Data Security & Storage

All passwords are encrypted with industry-standard PBKDF2 hashing algorithms. Database transaction records, audit pathways, and security controls are continuously checked to defend against unauthorized penetration.

4. Third Party Integration

If you link or sign in with third-party channels (like Google Authentication), we gather profile metrics in compliance with Google API standards. We do not sell or monetize personal customer details to external aggregators.
