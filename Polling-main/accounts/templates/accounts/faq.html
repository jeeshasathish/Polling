Frequently Asked Questions

Find instant answers to the most common questions regarding VoteHub account configuration, poll moderation, and result calculation.

How do I cast a vote on a poll?
Simply register and sign in to your dashboard. Browse the "All Active Surveys" feed, click "View" on any active poll, select your choice, and click "Cast Your Vote". Your response will be securely and instantly registered.
Are my survey votes anonymous?
Yes! We prioritize your privacy. While we verify your authorization level to ensure singular voting capabilities, individual survey response selections are strictly aggregated and anonymized in the dashboard statistics database.
Who can create and publish polls?
Only users with Admin or Super Admin privileges have access to administrative dashboards, poll configuration layouts, categories creation tools, and moderation buttons. If you require standard admin access, contact the platform Super Admin.
Can I close or edit a live poll?
Administrators can edit questions or choices for draft polls. Once a survey goes live (Active/Published), choices cannot be altered to preserve response authenticity, but admins can close or delete the poll at any time.
